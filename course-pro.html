Your Hardware

Your AI CEO Needs Its Own Computer.
Here's Why - and What to Get.

Your AI CEO is designed to run 24/7 - working between your conversations, running scheduled tasks, and staying ready. That requires a dedicated machine. Here's everything you need to know.

Why dedicated - not your main laptop:

→Your agent runs 24/7. Close your laptop, your agent goes dark. A dedicated machine stays on.
→Isolation. Your personal work doesn't interfere with your agent - and vice versa.
→Reliability. One machine, one job. No competing apps, no sleep interruptions, no battery shutdowns.
→Security. Your agent connects to your Stripe, Discord, and APIs. A dedicated, clean environment reduces exposure.
⭐ Recommended
Mac mini (M2 or newer)
Designed to run 24/7. Uses about as much power as a light bulb. Silent. Compact. macOS is the most stable environment for long-running processes and all your integrations. Starting at $599. This is what the program is built and tested on.
Windows Desktop or Laptop
Fully supported. Windows 10 and 11 both work. The program covers Windows setup at every step. Best on a dedicated desktop - if you use a laptop, disable sleep and keep it plugged in.
MacBook Pro or Air
Works, but not ideal as a dedicated machine. When the lid closes, the agent can stop. If you already own one and want to dedicate it, configure it to stay awake and plugged in - it'll work just fine.
Linux Mini PC
Excellent option for the tech-comfortable. Any mini PC running Ubuntu 22.04+ works great. Hardware requirements are minimal - 4GB RAM and an SSD handles it easily. Often cheaper than a Mac mini.
